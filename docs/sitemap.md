# Site Map & URL Hierarchy - OC Water Features

## Page URL Structure

- `/` - Homepage (Hero, Trust Bar, Services, About Preview, Projects, Testimonials, CTA)
- `/about` - About OC Water Features (Family history, values, industry experience since 1992)
- `/services` - Master Services Catalog
  - `/services/fountain-maintenance` - Fountain Maintenance & Cleaning
  - `/services/fountain-repair` - Fountain Mechanical & Structural Repair
  - `/services/pond-cleaning` - Koi Pond Cleaning & Ecosystem Care
  - `/services/pump-repair` - Pump Troubleshooting & Replacement
  - `/services/leak-detection` - Leak Detection & Water Loss Sealing
  - `/services/commercial-water-features` - HOA & Commercial Water Features
- `/projects` - Portfolio Gallery with category filters
- `/service-areas` - Orange County & Southern California cities served
- `/request-estimate` - Interactive 8-field Estimate Request Form
- `/contact` - Direct contact details & business hours
