# Local SEO Plan & Strategy - OC Water Features

## Target Geography & Keywords

Primary Region: Orange County and surrounding Southern California communities.

### Primary Keywords
- `fountain repair Orange County`
- `fountain maintenance Newport Beach`
- `koi pond cleaning Irvine`
- `water feature pump replacement SoCal`
- `waterfall leak detection Laguna Beach`
- `commercial water feature service Anaheim`

### On-Page SEO Architecture
1. **Title Tags**: Tailored per page with primary service + regional modifier + brand suffix.
2. **Meta Descriptions**: Compelling call-to-action copy incorporating local keywords.
3. **Structured Data**:
   - `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD schema injected into `<head>` across all pages.
   - Includes business name, service region, telephone, pricing level, and opening hours.
4. **App Router SEO Features**:
   - `sitemap.ts` generates `/sitemap.xml` automatically.
   - `robots.ts` generates `/robots.txt` automatically.
